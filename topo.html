Topology - I

Designed for first-year PhD students, this course provides a comprehensive introduction to Differential Topology, covering differentiability as well as integration on manifolds.

Course Coordinator Dr. Rama Mishra
Term & Schedule AUG 2026 (Next: Fall 2027)
Credits & Nature 4 Credits (LT - Lecture & Tutorial)
Eligibility Open to Semesters 13, 21

Pre-requisites

Foundational understanding of Point Set Topology.

Course Content & Syllabus

01

Smooth Structures & Maps

  • Smooth structures and smooth maps between manifolds.
  • Bump functions, Partitions of Unity, and Extension Lemma.
  • Tangent vectors and pushforwards.
02

Vector & Tangent Bundles

  • Tangent Bundles, vector fields, and vector bundles.
  • Bundle maps, sections, and cotangent bundles.
03

Submanifolds & Mappings

  • Inverse and Implicit Function Theorems.
  • Submanifolds, submersions, and level sets.
  • Statement and application of Sard's Theorem.
04

Vector Fields & Flows

  • Integral curves and flows of vector fields.
  • Lie bracket of vector fields.
  • Statement of Frobenius Theorem.
05

Differential Forms & Cohomology

  • Differential forms, wedge product, and exterior derivative.
  • Definition of De Rham Cohomology groups.
06

Integration & Stokes' Theorem

  • Orientation and Riemannian Volume form.
  • Integration on Manifolds using differential forms.
  • Stokes' Theorem on Manifolds.
