Algebra - I

Advanced theories of groups, rings, modules, category theory, and linear algebra essential for pure mathematics research.

Course Coordinator Dr. Rabeya Basu
Term & Schedule AUG 2026 (Next: Aug 2027)
Credits & Nature 4 Credits (LE - Lecture)
Eligibility Open to Semesters 13 & 21

Pre-requisites

BS-MS level foundational knowledge of Basic Group Theory, Rings and Modules, and Basic Linear Algebra.

Course Content & Syllabus

01

Groups

  • Examples, category of groups, Action of a group on a set.
  • Subgroups and isomorphism theorems.
  • Group actions: Permutation representations, action on itself by left multiplication, action on itself by conjugation.
02

Automorphisms & Sylow Theorems

  • Automorphisms: Inner automorphisms, automorphism groups of finite groups (dihedral, quaternions, cyclic).
  • Statement and application of Sylow's theorem, Direct and Semidirect products.
  • Simple groups, composition series, Jordan-Hölder Series, simplicity of An.
03

Category Theory

  • Foundations of Category Theory: Objects, morphisms, and functors.
04

Free Groups

  • Free groups: words, construction, and uniqueness.
  • Universal property, adjointness with forgetful functor.
  • Finitely generated and finitely presented groups.
05

Rings

  • Review of definitions: Integral domains, Euclidean domains, PID, UFD, fields.
  • Examples: Polynomial rings, Matrix rings, group rings.
  • Ideals and Quotient rings, Prime ideals and Maximal ideals.
  • Chinese Remainder Theorem.
  • Nilradical and Jacobson radical.
06

Modules

  • Definitions, ℤ-modules, and F[x]-modules.
  • Direct sums and free modules: construction and universal property.
07

Bilinear Forms

  • Symmetric forms, orthogonal bases, ordered fields, Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization, Sylvester's theorem.
  • Eigenvectors of linear maps, Spectral theorem (Hermitian, Unitary, Symmetric cases).
  • Structure theorem for alternating forms.
08

Tensors

  • Tensor products of modules and concrete examples.
  • Universal property, Adjointness with Hom.
  • Tensor product of homomorphisms, associativity, symmetry, and tensor product of algebras.
09

Symmetric and Exterior Algebras

  • Linear functions on tensor products of vector spaces, determinants.
  • Symmetric algebras and universal properties; Alternating algebras and universal properties.
  • Symmetric and alternating tensors.
10

Modules over a PID & Canonical Forms

  • Structure theorem of finitely generated modules over a Principal Ideal Domain (PID).
  • Canonical forms theory.
  • Rational Canonical Form.
  • Jordan Canonical Form.
